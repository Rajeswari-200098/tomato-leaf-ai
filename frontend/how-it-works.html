Preprocessing

The image is converted to RGB and resized to 224×224. Pixel values are scaled to [-1, 1], exactly as MobileNetV2 expects. This scaling is part of the saved model, so training and prediction always match.

Data augmentation

During training only: random flips, rotation, zoom and contrast changes make the model robust to different photo conditions. It is not applied at prediction time.

CNN with transfer learning

MobileNetV2 pretrained on ImageNet is used as a feature extractor. Its convolutional layers detect edges, textures and lesion patterns, then pooled features feed a dropout layer and a 10-way softmax classifier. The top backbone layers are fine-tuned.

Classification & confidence

Softmax outputs a probability per class. The highest is the prediction and its probability is the confidence. Low-confidence results are flagged.
